Same café, same average load every day. Customers arrive at random, and random arrivals clump: some days the clumps land on top of each other and never clear.

Why one clump of customers costs an hour

A clump leaves a backlog, and the barista works it off only in spare time. At 90% busy that is one minute in ten, so the next clump usually lands first.
