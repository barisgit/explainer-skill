    Deflate-raw ties for smallest on our browser baseline

    AlternativeIn the pageWhy
    deflate-raw in base64418 KBShipped: tied smallest for Chrome 103, Firefox 113 and Safari 16.4.
    gzip418 KBThe same size as deflate-raw.
    Brotli344 KBSmaller, but DecompressionStream does not read it in those baseline versions.
    Bytes as Latin-1 text470 KBLarger than base64 once the page is UTF-8.
    Island bundle, plain60 to 300 KBMust run before the views and steps scripts; waiting to unpack would delay them.
    CSS, plainCSS cannot wait for a script.

    A browser without deflate-raw support keeps the drawn charts

    The deflate-raw format needs Chrome 103, Firefox 113 or Safari 16.4. On older browsers the unpack fails and logs the failure. The charts pack drew stay; the live ones that replace them never mount.
