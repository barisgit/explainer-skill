Home battery payback by size

The first 5 kWh pays for itself in 10 years. The second would take 62 years. Three quotes for a 6 kW roof on a four-person house: 5 kWh for €4,200, 10 kWh for €7,900, 15 kWh for €11,200.

    A July night

    A July night needs 3.8 kWh: what the house draws between the roof falling short in the evening and covering it again in the morning. Whatever the battery holds beyond it stays in the battery.

    The second 5 kWh

    It mostly works in spring and autumn. In summer the first 5 kWh usually carries the night, and in winter the roof hardly fills it. The second block takes over on longer nights after a sunny day.
