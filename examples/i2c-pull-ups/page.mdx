# The {N.found} pull-ups take *{N.foundTr} ns* to raise the line. At 400 kHz the bus allows {N.fastTr}.

A {N.vdd} V bus with about {N.cb} pF on each line, timed from 0.3 to 0.7 VDD as the I²C specification times it.

<Opening />

## Nothing drives the line high but a resistor

<div class="beside">
<div>

The <span data-ref="U1">MCU</span> and the <span data-ref="U2">sensor</span> can only pull <span data-ref="sda">SDA</span> and <span data-ref="scl">SCL</span> low. When they let go, <span data-ref="R_SDA">R<sub>SDA</sub></span> has to charge the line's capacitance <span data-ref="C_SDA">C<sub>SDA</sub></span> on its own, and the time that takes grows with the product of the two.

At 100 kHz an {N.foundTr} ns edge fits inside the {N.standardTr} ns Standard-mode allows. At 400 kHz the edge outlasts the {N.fastTr} ns the specification allows, so SDA may still be under 0.7 VDD, the lowest level UM10204 counts as high (Table 10), when the sensor reads it.

</div>

```schematic
U1 ic MCU @0,0 right SDA=sda SCL=scl
U2 ic Sensor SDA@U1.SDA 6,0 left SDA=sda SCL=scl
R_SDA vbus sda 10k b@U1.SDA 2,-3 v
R_SCL vbus scl 10k a@R_SDA.a 3,0 v
C_SDA sda 0 100 pF a@U1.SDA 2,5 v
C_SCL scl 0 100 pF a@C_SDA.a 3,0 v
VDD port vbus 3.3 V @R_SDA.a -4,-2
```

</div>

## Any standard value from {N.low} to {N.high} fits; {N.fix} leaves room

<Explore label="Rise time of each E12 pull-up from 680 Ω to 10 kΩ against the 300 ns limit" controls={[{ id: "r", label: "Pull-up", choice: VALUES, value: "2200", axis: true }]} frame={stripFrame} />

{N.high} only just fits, at {N.highTr} ns, if the {N.cb} pF estimate is right. {N.fix} still fits up to {N.fixCbMax} pF.

## Where 0.8473 comes from

Released at $t = 0$, the line charges towards VDD as $V(t) = V_{DD}\,(1 - e^{-t/RC})$. It crosses 0.3 VDD at $t_1 = RC \ln\frac{1}{0.7}$ and 0.7 VDD at $t_2 = RC \ln\frac{1}{0.3}$, so

$$
\begin{aligned} t_r &= t_2 - t_1 = RC \ln\frac{0.7}{0.3} = 0.8473\,R\,C_b \\ &= 0.8473 \cdot 10\,\text{k}\Omega \cdot 100\,\text{pF} = 847\,\text{ns} \end{aligned}
$$

The bounds follow: $R_{max} = t_r / (0.8473\,C_b)$, which is {N.fastRMax} kΩ at 400 kHz, and $R_{min} = (V_{DD} - 0.4\,\text{V}) / I_{OL}$, which is {N.fastRMin} Ω assuming only the minimum guaranteed sink of 3 mA at 0.4 V.
