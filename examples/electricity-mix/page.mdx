# Wind and solar added <em>{fmt(WS)} TWh</em> in {Y1}. Total generation added {fmt(TOTAL)}.

World electricity generation, change on the year before, in TWh. Source: Our World in Data, from Ember and the Energy Institute.

## Sources

Our World in Data, [energy dataset](https://github.com/owid/energy-data) (CC BY 4.0), electricity columns from [Ember](https://ember-energy.org/data/yearly-electricity-data/) and the [Energy Institute](https://www.energyinst.org/statistical-review/), 2000 to {Y1}. Figures are generation, not consumption, and each is the change from the year before, so weather moves them: a wet year lifts hydro. A blank cell counts as zero only where the other sources already add up to the reported total; countries without both years are left out of the counts.
