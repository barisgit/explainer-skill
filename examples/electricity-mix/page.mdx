## Sources

Our World in Data, [energy dataset](https://github.com/owid/energy-data): the legacy `owid-energy-data.csv` downloaded 22 October 2026, keeping 2000 to {Y1}; partial 2025 is left out. The upstream revision and provider editions were not recorded.

The electricity columns come from [Ember, Yearly Electricity Data](https://ember-energy.org/data/yearly-electricity-data/) ([CC BY 4.0](https://ember-energy.org/creative-commons/)) and, for some places and years, the [Energy Institute, Statistical Review of World Energy](https://www.energyinst.org/statistical-review/). OWID's CC BY 4.0 covers its own contributions; third-party data retain their providers' terms. The Energy Institute's terms are its own, not CC BY: attribution is required and extensive reproduction needs its permission. Check the applicable terms before reusing this extract.

Figures are generation, not consumption, and each is the change from the year before, so weather moves them: a wet year lifts hydro. A blank cell counts as zero only where the other sources already add up to the reported total; countries without both years are left out of the counts.
